# Lingo Specs — Demo Video Script (Spectacles Community Challenge)

Target: **≤30 seconds**, "New Lens" category. Judges reward multi-feature depth, daily usefulness and polish — show the SPATIAL magic fast, one feature per beat, no menus lingering on screen.

## Shot list (30s)

### 0–4s — Hook: the room becomes vocabulary
- Already in SCAN mode. Tap ESCANEAR in a tidy, high-contrast room with 4–5 clear objects.
- Cards pop onto the real objects with leader lines and anchor dots.
- Voice-over: "Lingo Specs turns your room into your vocabulary."

### 4–10s — Objects talk to you (spatial audio)
- Walk toward a card, tap LISTEN. The object speaks its word + phrase **from where it sits** (keep head motion so the spatial pan is audible in the capture).
- Hold to talk, pronounce it, show the ✓ verdict on the card + XP tick.
- Voice-over: "Every object teaches you its own name — and listens back."

### 10–17s — Word Hunt
- Tap CAZA DE PALABRAS. The coach asks "¿Dónde está…?" — walk, find the right card among several, tap it, celebrate sparkle + XP.
- Voice-over: "Play Word Hunt: find the word hiding in your own home."

### 17–23s — It knows your rooms
- Walk into a second room; the first room's cards vanish behind the real wall (occlusion beat — brief!).
- Scan; show the room label and the review nudge ("¡N palabras de cocina te esperan!").
- Voice-over: "It remembers what lives in each room, and coaches you where you stand."

### 23–30s — Library + close
- Flash the wrist glossary (thumbnail art, play button) and 2s of the kawaii quiz.
- End card: Lingo Specs logo + "Six languages. Your world."

## Recording checklist
- Capture from Spectacles directly (Snap capture) for true FOV; record room-tone audio so the spatial TTS pan survives.
- One take per beat; cut hard — no UI idle time. 30s is a HARD cap.
- High-contrast environment, 4–5 recognizable objects, good light.
- One successful pronunciation ✓ and one Word Hunt find on camera.
- Never show the Remote Service Gateway token panel or any credentials.
- Upload public link (Drive/Dropbox), paste into SUBMISSION.md and the challenge form; publish the Lens ≥3 days before the deadline and post on r/Spectacles.
